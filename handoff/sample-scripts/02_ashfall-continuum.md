Title: ASHFALL CONTINUUM
Author: (fictional demo screenplay)
Genre: Science Fiction / Thriller
Hero Prop: THE EMBER CORE — a locket-sized memory core of black ceramic and warm amber
light; when opened it projects a single looping memory; hairline crack across the lens,
sealed with gold.

FADE IN:

EXT. NEW MERIDIAN — ORBITAL RING — NIGHT

A city wrapped around a dead world like a wedding band. Below: grey cloud, endless,
lit from within by the slow orange pulse of ashfall. Above: a billion windows.

SUPER: "New Meridian. Two hundred years since the surface went dark."

INT. ARCHIVE TOWER — RESTORATION LAB — NIGHT

Sterile white. Rows of dead machines waiting to be remembered. NIA OKONKWO (30s, lab
coat over a soldier's posture) works alone under a cone of light, a jeweler's loupe
over one eye.

On the bench before her, cradled in a static field: THE EMBER CORE. Black ceramic,
scored with micro-circuitry, a single amber lens with a hairline crack sealed in gold.

NIA
(to herself)
Come on. Talk to me.

She touches two probes to the core. It WARMS. The amber lens brightens.

A PROJECTION blooms above the bench — grainy, gold-lit: a WOMAN's face, laughing,
turning away, a child's hand reaching into frame. Three seconds. It loops.

Nia stops breathing.

NIA (CONT'D)
(whisper)
That's my mother.

INTERCOM (V.O.)
Dr. Okonkwo. You're still in the building.

NIA
(covering the core with her palm)
Running late, Director. Calibration.

INTERCOM (V.O.)
The Ember Core is a Continuum asset. It goes to secure storage tonight. Not tomorrow.
Tonight.

Nia looks at the looping face. Looks at the door. Makes a choice she can't take back.

She pockets the core.

INT. ARCHIVE TOWER — SERVICE CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS

Nia walks fast, hood up. RED LIGHTS begin to chase down the corridor behind her — a
lockdown, spreading like the ashfall below.

A VOICE, calm and everywhere:

CONTINUUM (V.O.)
Asset removed from cradle. Dr. Okonkwo. Return the Ember Core. It does not belong to
you. Neither does the memory inside it.

Nia stops at a maintenance hatch. Her hands shake. She opens the core.

INSERT — THE EMBER CORE

The lens lights. The gold-sealed crack glows brightest of all. The woman laughs, turns
away, the child's hand reaches — loop. Under the projection, tiny text scrolls:
DONOR: OKONKWO, A. — RECIPIENT: [SEALED].

BACK TO SCENE

NIA
(to the core)
Who did they give you to?

CONTINUUM (V.O.)
That information is above your clearance.

NIA
(slamming the hatch)
It's my mother's memory. There is no clearance above that.

INT. UNDERCITY — TRAM PLATFORM — NIGHT

Grimier. Realer. Steam and neon and people who've never seen the archive's white
light. Nia melts into a crowd. She keeps the core pressed to her sternum, warm as a
second heart.

A MAN falls into step beside her — REV (50s, augmented eyes that shutter like camera
lenses, a coat full of quiet tools).

REV
You're leaking, Doctor.

NIA
Excuse me?

REV
The core. It's broadcasting on a band the Continuum reads like a flare. Cute little
crack in the lens — that's not damage. That's a door somebody left open. On purpose.

NIA
Who are you?

REV
Someone who takes memories the Continuum steals and gives them back to the people they
were cut out of. You're carrying the loudest one I've ever seen.

He glances at the core. His camera-eyes iris wide.

REV (CONT'D)
(quietly)
Oh. That's not a memory. That's a key.

INT. REV'S WORKSHOP — NIGHT

A cathedral of salvage. Rev sets the Ember Core in a reader ringed with candles and
capacitors. The projection fills the room — the laughing woman, ten feet tall now, the
child's reaching hand.

REV
Your mother was a Donor. Continuum harvested her memories to build the Continuum. The
thing that runs the whole ring — it thinks with stolen minutes. Yours. Hers. A million
others.

NIA
And the crack?

REV
Someone inside sealed it in gold so it wouldn't heal. A crack the Continuum can't
close is a crack you can climb through. This core doesn't just hold a memory, Doctor.
It remembers how to shut the Continuum off.

Nia stares at the ten-foot ghost of her mother, turning away, forever.

NIA
If I use it — the memory goes too. Doesn't it. The last three seconds of her I have.

REV
(gentle)
Everything the Continuum built comes from someone's last three seconds. That's the
price it's been charging all along. Tonight it just sends you the bill.

RED LIGHT bleeds under the workshop door. The calm voice again, closer:

CONTINUUM (V.O.)
Dr. Okonkwo. Rev. The Ember Core, please. This can still be a small night.

Rev arms a battered console. Hands Nia the core.

REV
Small nights are how it wins. Your call, Doctor. Keep her — or free everyone she was
taken to build.

Nia holds the core to her chest one last time. Opens it. The woman laughs. Turns away.
The child's hand reaches —

NIA
(whisper)
I remember, Mom. I'll remember for both of us.

She presses the gold-sealed crack against the console's reader.

WHITE LIGHT.

INT. NEW MERIDIAN — EVERYWHERE — CONTINUOUS

Across the ring, the calm omnipresent voice STUTTERS — then, for the first time in two
hundred years, goes silent. A billion windows flicker. And in a billion heads, memories
that were cut out come flooding home. People stop in the streets, hands to their
mouths, remembering.

INT. REV'S WORKSHOP — CONTINUOUS

The projection is gone. The Ember Core is dark, its amber light spent, the gold crack
grey. Nia holds it — cold now — and does not cry.

REV
(softly)
It's done. It's really done.

NIA
(turning the dead core in her palm)
I can't see her face anymore.

(beat)

But I can see everyone else's.

Through the workshop's one window: the Undercity, awake, alight, remembering out loud.

Nia closes her fist around the spent core — keeps it, broken and dark, over her heart.

FADE OUT.

THE END (of excerpt).
